## Short description (the one line under the title)

A child carries her village's last coal home through a frozen world, seen through frosted glass.

## Description

The cold came, and every hearth in the village went out, except the coal one child carried.

Walk her home through five stages of a frozen world: a still, dark plain, a windswept ridge, a mountain that will not stay quiet, a black lake of thin ice, and the road back to the village. Her coal is all the warmth she has. Every pulse of it can thaw what the frost has taken (a tree, a lantern, a frozen villager), but every pulse costs warmth she needs to stay alive.

- **One verb:** pulse warmth in time with her heartbeat.
- **Choices that stay with you:** warm the light or the one beside it, the ridge or the cave, dig someone out or keep moving. Who you bring home lights a window in the village at the end.
- **Four endings,** greener the more of the world you thaw.
- **About 12 minutes,** including a one-minute prologue set to the jam's theme.
- Your walk is saved in the browser. Pick any stage you have reached from the title.

## Controls

- **Walk:** arrows or A / D, or the left stick
- **Pulse:** hold Space or A. Press it in time with her heartbeat for a stronger pulse.
- **The low way at a cairn:** down
- **Pause:** Esc, P or Start
- **Mute:** M
- **Volume:** - and +
- **Screen shake:** X
- **Full screen:** F
- **Touch:** hold the left or right of the screen to walk, the middle to pulse, swipe down for the low way. Hold your phone sideways.

Best with sound on.
