# Audio credits

The jam theme belongs to its composer. Every other recording is human-made and released under CC0 (public domain); credit is not required, but given gladly.

The recordings were only cut, leveled and faded for the game. The melt drips and the ember crackle also have the low rumble filtered out so the drips and crackles come forward, and the game plays some of them slightly faster or slower so repeats vary.

- **Theme:** thatgamejam #02 theme by Jason Grace. Used unmodified, only inside this jam entry, as the jam rules require.

## Music

The tracks were re-encoded to a smaller file size and leveled to the same loudness, nothing else.

- **Stage music:** "Long Winter" by Indieteur, OpenGameArt, 2018. https://opengameart.org/content/long-winter
  - File: `music/long-winter.mp3`
- **When she is close to freezing:** "Esther" by Kistol, OpenGameArt, 2014. https://opengameart.org/content/esther
  - File: `music/esther.mp3`
- **Rest at the fire, and the prologue if the theme cannot play:** "November Snow" by cynicmusic (The Cynic Project, cynicmusic.com, pixelsphere.org), OpenGameArt, 2017. https://opengameart.org/content/november-snow
  - File: `music/november-snow.mp3`

## Sound effects

- **Wind (ambient):** "Winter wind whistling through window.mp3" by audible-edge, Freesound, 2009. https://freesound.org/people/audible-edge/sounds/69512/
  - File: `sfx/wind-window.mp3`
- **Wind (hazards):** "Howling winter storm ambient sounds" by DBlover, Freesound, 2020. https://freesound.org/people/DBlover/sounds/505999/
  - File: `sfx/wind-storm.mp3`
  - Also the mountain's rumble in Stage 3: the same recording played at half speed, an octave lower.
- **Ice cracking:** "Ice Cracks" by karlis.stigis, Freesound, 2012. https://freesound.org/people/karlis.stigis/sounds/168712/
  - Files: `sfx/ice-crack-1.mp3` to `sfx/ice-crack-6.mp3`, short cuts from the one recording.
  - Also played slower and lower for avalanches, frost quakes and breaking through the ice, and faster for icicles shattering and thin ice creaking.
- **Footsteps:** "Footstep in the snow_04 [RAW]" by cabled_mess, Freesound, 2017. https://freesound.org/people/cabled_mess/sounds/384424/
  - Files: `sfx/snow-step-1.mp3` and `sfx/snow-step-2.mp3`, the two crunches in the one step.
- **Fire at the stage end:** "Fire crackling in fireplace" by Davor, Freesound, 2017. https://freesound.org/people/Davor/sounds/382616/
  - File: `sfx/fire.mp3`
- **Heartbeat:** "SLOW HEARTBEAT" by daandraait, Freesound, 2014. https://freesound.org/people/daandraait/sounds/249716/
  - Files: `sfx/heartbeat-1.mp3` to `sfx/heartbeat-4.mp3`, single beats from the one recording.
- **Pulse (her breath on the coal):** "Man Blowing Candle Out.wav" by bbrocer, Freesound, 2017. https://freesound.org/people/bbrocer/sounds/382667/
  - Files: `sfx/pulse-1.mp3` to `sfx/pulse-3.mp3`, three gentle blows.
- **Melting:** "Snow Dripping" by LilMati, Freesound, 2020. https://freesound.org/people/LilMati/sounds/514967/
  - Files: `sfx/melt-1.mp3` to `sfx/melt-3.mp3`.

- **Refreezing:** "Winter night - frozen trees" by traus, Freesound, 2016. https://freesound.org/people/traus/sounds/370266/

- **Hums of frozen things:** "Wine Glass Ringing/Singing" by BJCurlee, Freesound, 2017. https://freesound.org/people/BJCurlee/sounds/396293/
